# P4 Scope & Non-goals

## Included
- Linkareer recruitment postings.
- 2020-01 through 2026-07.
- Reviewed AI·IT cohort.
- Posting/track/section/source-block/chunk/fact lineage.
- RQ1, RQ2-A, RQ2-B.
- NCS official unit/level/band as reference.
- Contextual/qualitative evidence as sidecars.

## Excluded from primary denominator
- Work24 recruitment universe.
- Other commercial job platforms.
- KOSIS/BOK employment statistics.
- Interview respondents.
- Company official pages.

## Forbidden interpretations
- “AI 때문에 신입채용이 줄었다.”
- “기업이 AI로 신입을 인턴으로 대체했다.”
- “NCS level이 높으므로 해당 지원자의 숙련도가 높다.”

## Publication language
Allowed: 기술통계, 기간/집단 차이, 패턴, 상관, 조건부 연관성.
Causal wording requires an independently identified causal design.
